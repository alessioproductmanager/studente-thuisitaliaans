CAPITOLO 1

Il libro della memoria

The Book of Memory

Nel libro della mia memoria, nella parte prima della quale poco si potrebbe leggere, si trova una rubrica che dice: Incipit vita nova — comincia la vita nuova.1 Sotto quella rubrica stanno scritte le…

memoria = memory · quaderno = notebook · anima = soul

1 Il latino Incipit vita nova («comincia la vita nuova») dà il titolo all’opera. Nei manoscritti medievali le «rubriche» erano i titoli scritti in rosso — ruber, in latino. Dante immagina la propria memoria come un codice miniato: la vita, per lui, è già un libro prima di essere scritta.

The plot of La Vita Nuova

Florence. Dante is nine years old when he first sees Beatrice, and from that day — he writes — «Love rules my soul». A denied greeting, a terrible dream, sonnets sent to his poet friends: the Vita Nuova weaves prose and verse to tell, chapter by chapter, how a love is born and transformed.

When Beatrice dies, grief becomes the hardest test and the poetry changes nature. The book closes with a promise worth all of Italian literature: to say of her «what has never been said of any woman» — the promise that would become the Divine Comedy.

Why read La Vita Nuova to learn Italian

Prose and poetry explain each other: Dante tells you the occasion, then shows the sonnet born from it — the most natural way to approach Italian poetry without fear. And you meet the father of the language in his most intimate, readable book.

Language focus: what you practise

In this level-B2 edition you mainly practise the imperfect and past subjunctive, hypothetical clauses of possibility, the passive and indirect speech. The vocabulary revolves around love, poetry and dreams: the key words, with translations, are in the glossary below and recur throughout the text, so you learn them in context.

About the author: Dante Alighieri

Dante Alighieri (1265–1321), from Florence, is the father of the Italian language. The Vita Nuova, written in his youth, is the laboratory of love and poetry from which, years later, the Divine Comedy would be born.

What level of Italian do I need to read La Vita Nuova?

Is this Dante Alighieri's original text?

No, it is an adaptation: the plot and spirit of Dante Alighieri's original remain, but vocabulary and syntax are rewritten for level B2, with footnotes, a final glossary and comprehension questions at the end of every chapter.
