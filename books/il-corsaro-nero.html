CAPITOLO 1

Il canotto nella notte

The Boat in the Night

Era notte. Il mare era nero come l'inchiostro.

Una barca piccola andava avanti piano. Dentro c'erano due uomini.

Remavano forte, e guardavano indietro con paura.

Poi una voce ha gridato dal buio:

«Uomini della barca! Fermi! O vi mando a fondo!»

I due si sono fermati subito. Davanti a loro c'era una grande ombra: una nave.

Un corsaro arriva così: senza rumore. Ha le vele nere e nessuna bandiera. E chi lo sente gridare ha già perso.1

◆

I due uomini si chiamavano Carmaux e Wan Stiller.

corsaro = corsair · vela = sail · bandiera = flag · marinaio = sailor · equipaggio = crew · lutto = mourning

1 Salgari comincia sempre così: buio, mare, e una voce che grida un ordine. Non ti dice dove sei né chi sono: te lo fa capire dopo. Prima devi avere paura, poi capirai.

The plot of Il corsaro nero

The Caribbean, seventeenth century. Emilio di Roccabruna, lord of Ventimiglia, has become the Black Corsair for one purpose only: to avenge his brothers, killed by the traitor Wan Guld, governor of Maracaibo. With his ship, the Folgore, he spreads terror among the Spanish galleons.

But fate places Honorata in his path, a mysterious and beautiful woman hiding a terrible secret. Between boardings, jungles and blood oaths, Salgari's most famous adventure races on to the very last page.

Why read Il corsaro nero to learn Italian

The pace of adventure makes you read page after page without noticing: ideal for building reading stamina at A2. Short sentences full of movement verbs and sea vocabulary make every scene easy to visualise.

Language focus: what you practise

In this level-A2 edition you mainly practise imperfetto versus passato prossimo, the simple future, direct and indirect pronouns. The vocabulary revolves around the sea, adventure and action: the key words, with translations, are in the glossary below and recur throughout the text, so you learn them in context.

About the author: Emilio Salgari

Emilio Salgari (1862–1911) wrote more than eighty adventure novels while hardly ever travelling: Malaysian pirates, Caribbean corsairs, tigers and sailing ships all born from his imagination and entire libraries of atlases.

Frequently asked questions

What level of Italian do I need to read Il corsaro nero?

This edition of Il corsaro nero is rewritten for CEFR level A2. A2 is solid elementary Italian: still simple sentences, but with the imperfetto, the future tense and a wider vocabulary. This reader suits learners with a few months of Italian who want to finish their first complete story.

Is this Emilio Salgari's original text?

No, it is an adaptation: the plot and spirit of Emilio Salgari's original remain, but vocabulary and syntax are rewritten for level A2, with footnotes, a final glossary and comprehension questions at the end of every chapter.
