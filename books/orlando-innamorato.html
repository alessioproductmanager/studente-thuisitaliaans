La festa di Pentecoste

The Feast of Pentecost

Era primavera, e a Parigi c'era una grande festa.

Il re Carlo Magno aveva invitato tutti i cavalieri del mondo.

Erano arrivati cristiani e saraceni, amici e nemici. Per la festa, la guerra si fermava.

Nelle strade c'era musica. Sui palazzi c'erano i colori di cento regni.

Il re ha annunciato un grande torneo: i migliori uomini del mondo, uno contro l'altro, per gioco.1

◆

Alla tavola del re sedevano i paladini: i dodici migliori cavalieri di Francia.

Il primo era Orlando, nipote del re. Nessuna spada poteva ferirlo. La sua si chiamava Durindana, ed …

cavaliere = knight · saraceno = Saracen · guerra = war · torneo = tournament · paladino = paladin · spada = sword

1 Boiardo comincia con una cosa impossibile: cristiani e saraceni che fanno festa insieme, in piena guerra. Non è storia, è fiaba — e te lo dice subito. In questo mondo le regole le fa il racconto.

The plot of L'Orlando innamorato

Paris. King Charlemagne has invited every knight in the world — friends and enemies — to a great tournament. Then a girl nobody has ever seen walks into the hall: her name is Angelica, she comes from the East, and she is the most beautiful woman in the world. Within one evening, every knight is in love. Even Orlando, the strongest warrior alive, who has never loved anyone.

What follows is a chase across the world: enchanted forests, magic fountains that swap love and hate, a golden lance that never loses, giants, wizards and dragons — while armies larger than any city come from the sea. The poem that taught Italy how to tell adventures, openly continued by the Orlando furioso.

Why read L'Orlando innamorato to learn Italian

Fantasy adventure before fantasy existed: duels, wizards and dragons keep the pace racing, and at A2 pace is everything. The narrative structures repeat (challenge → duel → spell) and become natural scaffolding for comprehension.

Language focus: what you practise

In this level-A2 edition you mainly practise imperfetto versus passato prossimo, the simple future, direct and indirect pronouns. The vocabulary revolves around knights, magic and adventure: the key words, with translations, are in the glossary below and recur throughout the text, so you learn them in context.

About the author: Matteo Maria Boiardo

Matteo Maria Boiardo (1441–1494), count of Scandiano and poet at the court of Ferrara, invented the great Italian chivalric romance with L'Orlando innamorato. His poem breaks off in mid-verse, as «Italy goes up in flames» in the French invasion.

What level of Italian do I need to read L'Orlando innamorato?

This edition of L'Orlando innamorato is rewritten for CEFR level A2. A2 is solid elementary Italian: still simple sentences, but with the imperfetto, the future tense and a wider vocabulary. This reader suits learners with a few months of Italian who want to finish their first complete story.

Is this Matteo Maria Boiardo's original text?

No, it is an adaptation: the plot and spirit of Matteo Maria Boiardo's original remain, but vocabulary and syntax are rewritten for level A2, with footnotes, a final glossary and comprehension questions at the end of every chapter.
