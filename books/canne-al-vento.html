CAPITOLO 1

L’argine del podere

The Embankment

Tutto il giorno Efix aveva lavorato. Costruiva un argine di terra e pietre lungo il fiume, in fondo al podere delle dame Pintor.

Era un lavoro vecchio di anni. Ogni primavera il fiume cresceva e rompeva il muro.1 Ogni primavera Efix lo rifaceva, da solo, con le mani.

argine = embankment · podere = smallholding · servo = servant

1 Il fiume che rompe sempre l’argine è il primo simbolo del libro: c’è una forza che l’uomo non può fermare, e che l’uomo continua a combattere lo stesso.

The plot of Canne al vento

In a Sardinian village live the three Pintor sisters, impoverished nobility, and their servant Efix, who works their land to atone for a secret guilt that binds him to the family forever. The arrival of their young nephew Giacinto shatters a balance held for years.

Amid village feasts, debts and superstitions, the characters bend to fate «like reeds in the wind». Deledda's most famous novel is a song of guilt, atonement and the mysterious power of her Sardinia.

Why read Canne al vento to learn Italian

The descriptions of Sardinia are a goldmine of adjectives and descriptive imperfetto, and Deledda's rhythmic prose is beautiful to read aloud. The vocabulary of traditions, feasts and nature opens a window onto deep Italian culture.

About the author: Grazia Deledda

Grazia Deledda (1871–1936), born in Nuoro, was the first Italian woman to win the Nobel Prize in Literature, in 1926. In her novels Sardinia becomes a universal landscape of passion and fate.

Frequently asked questions

What level of Italian do I need to read Canne al vento?

This edition of Canne al vento is rewritten for CEFR level B1. B1 is the threshold of independence: longer sentences, the present subjunctive, rich everyday vocabulary. This reader suits intermediate students and anyone preparing a B1 certification (CILS, CELI, PLIDA).

Is this Grazia Deledda's original text?

No, it is an adaptation: the plot and spirit of Grazia Deledda's original remain, but vocabulary and syntax are rewritten for level B1, with footnotes, a final glossary and comprehension questions at the end of every chapter.
