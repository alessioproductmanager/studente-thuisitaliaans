Frequently asked questions about Genova

How many Italian language schools are there in Genova?
We have selected 2, with courses from 20 to 20 hours a week. They are all compared above.
How do I choose between the schools in Genova?
Look at three numbers, not the price alone. The real lesson length: 20 hours of 45 minutes are 15 actual hours, of 60 minutes they are 20. How many people are really in the class in high season, not the stated maximum. And the total including enrolment, materials and accommodation, not the weekly rate.
Which schools in Genova are exam centres?
No school in Genova is an exam centre: you sit the exam at a nearby venue, and the school helps you register.
Do the schools in Genova help you find accommodation?
Yes, all 2 of them. Host family, shared flat or residence: the host family is the most useful for the language, because it makes you speak Italian every evening.
How much does an Italian course in Genova cost?
Rates vary a lot and should be asked of the school. When you do, ask for the total: a one-off enrolment fee is almost always added to the weekly rate, usually 50–90 euros in Italy, and books, accommodation, cultural activities and exam fees may be charged separately.

The labels
ASILSItalian association of schools of Italian as a second language; quality audited by an independent certifier.
EDUITALIAAssociation of accredited schools and universities promoting study in Italy.
AILAccademia Italiana di Lingua: school network with its own Firenze Italian exams.
IALCInternational association of independent, inspected language schools.
BildungsurlaubGerman recognition: the course qualifies for paid educational leave.
CILSOfficial certificate of the University for Foreigners of Siena.
CELIOfficial certificate of the University for Foreigners of Perugia.
PLIDAOfficial certificate of the Società Dante Alighieri.
CSNSwedish student-finance body: the course qualifies for CSN study grants.
LICETAssociation of Italian language schools with shared quality standards and peer inspections.
