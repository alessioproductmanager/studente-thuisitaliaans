Scuola Leonardo da Vinci Firenze

Scuola Leonardo da Vinci Firenze is an Italian language school for foreigners in Firenze (Toscana): intensive courses of about 20 hours per week plus lighter options, usually starting every Monday. The school also helps you find accommodation. Below you will find the key facts, a mini guide to Firenze to plan your stay, and a form to write to the school directly.
