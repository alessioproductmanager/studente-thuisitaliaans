Business Italian

For people working with Italian clients, suppliers or colleagues who want to get past pleasantries: negotiating, presenting, emailing, phoning, and understanding the cultural codes around all of it.

Take the first step →︎

What we cover

Your own professional vocabulary first: I build the lexicon around your sector, not around a generic textbook. Alongside that: email registers from formal to informal, phone calls, following and interrupting in meetings, and presenting without reading from your notes.

The cultural side

Most misunderstandings with Italian partners aren't about language but about register: how direct you can be, when 'ci sentiamo' is an appointment and when it isn't, how a decision actually gets made. We cover that explicitly.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to already speak Italian?

No. Starting from zero is fine; we then build the business vocabulary in parallel with the basics.

Can my employer cover this?

Often yes, through the training budget. I issue an invoice with a KVK number and can supply whatever else your company needs.

Do you teach at our office?

Yes, between Ede and Amsterdam. Beyond that we work online.

What does a business track cost?

It depends on the number of participants, the frequency and the location. I'll put together a proposal after a no-obligation intake.

About the lessons and prices

Lessons are taught by Alessio, a teacher of Italian as a foreign language with two ITALS master’s degrees in teaching Italian as a second language (Università Ca’ Foscari in Venice and Università Giustino Fortunato in Benevento), teaching online and in person between Ede and Amsterdam since 2019. Prices are per person and include VAT where it applies; location, times and availability are agreed before we start. Thuis Italiaans is a sole proprietorship registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce. If I have no availability in a given period, I will put you in touch with another highly qualified teacher of Italian as a foreign language.
