Pricing

I don't work with fixed packages or subscriptions.

Take the first step →︎

Roughly what a lesson costs

Lessons start at €50 per hour per person. Online is a bit lower, since there's no travel involved. If you learn as a pair or in a small group, the per-person rate drops as the number of participants grows — I put together a tailored proposal depending on the participants and the frequency. Business Italian and tracks built on specific request — your own professional vocabulary, documents, or a fixed deadline — are also quoted individually. The final rate depends on your situation, your level and the distance I travel.

Why there's no price list

Because a price list implies something untrue: that a lesson is the same for everybody. An hour of conversation with an advanced learner and a 45-minute children's lesson at someone's home are not the same work. I set the rate around your situation — how often you come, whether you learn alone or as a pair, in person or online, and what you can comfortably carry.

What shapes the rate

Six things: frequency, group size, in person versus online, lesson length, your level and — for lessons at your place — the distance I travel. Online is lower than in person because there's no travel; learning more often costs less per lesson than occasional sessions.

Frequently asked questions

Can I afford this?

Just tell me honestly what works for you. I teach learners on very different budgets and in practice we almost always find a way — less frequent lessons, shorter lessons, or learning as a pair.

Does the first lesson cost anything?

No. The introduction is free and without obligation: we establish your level, your goal, and whether we get along.

Am I tied to anything?

No. No subscription, no minimum number of lessons, no notice period.

Do I get an invoice?

Yes, with a KVK number. Useful if your employer is contributing.

Book a free intake

Tell me what you want to achieve. We'll look at your level, pace and price together — no obligation.

About the lessons and prices

Lessons are taught by Alessio, a teacher of Italian as a foreign language with two ITALS master’s degrees in teaching Italian as a second language (Università Ca’ Foscari in Venice and Università Giustino Fortunato in Benevento), teaching online and in person between Ede and Amsterdam since 2019. Prices are per person and include VAT where it applies; location, times and availability are agreed before we start.
