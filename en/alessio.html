Who writes this site

Alessio, a teacher of Italian as a foreign language

I teach online and in person between Ede and Amsterdam. This site, the Ti app and the graded reader series are mine, built one at a time, to answer problems I kept seeing in lessons.

What I do

I started as a pastime, and then it became my work. What kept me here is not the grammar: it is that I like talking to people, listening to them and understanding where they come from.

An hour of lesson is not only an hour of language. It is an exchange, personal, professional and cultural, and both of us grow in it: by the end I have learned something too, and I do not say that to be polite.

That is why in class I speak little and let you speak. Grammar arrives when it is needed, and when it is needed we do it properly; but the time goes on your life and your world, because that is what you will want to talk about in Italian with the people around you, not prepositions.

I work mostly with adults: people moving to Italy, people preparing the B1 certificate for citizenship, people who need it for work, and people who simply enjoy it.

Around the lessons I have built three things, each from a concrete problem. The Ti app, because seven days pass between one lesson and the next and a language does not wait. Twenty-five graded readers, because my students kept stopping on page three of a real novel. A free level test, because almost nobody guesses their own level and almost everybody builds a study plan on that guess.

Training

Teaching Italian to people who live in Italy and teaching it to people who study it from abroad are two different jobs, and they have two names: L2 and LS. I studied one master for each.

L2Studying while living in ItalyInput all day long. What is missing is correction.Master, Università Giustino Fortunato
LSStudying from abroadCorrection is there. What is missing is input.ITALS Master, Ca’ Foscari, LS track

The ITALS master at Ca’ Foscari (2023/2024) I took on the LS track, the one for Italian taught abroad: that is the situation of my students in the Netherlands. The Giustino Fortunato master (2022/2023) is on Italian as L2, for people who have the language around them all day, and it is built on the communicative approach: not transferring rules, but building the ability to use the language. I do not list them to fill a line. They are why this site cites the Common European Framework in full rather than by initials, why Nation’s 98% threshold sits behind the graded readers, and why the level test measures four separate skills instead of handing you one letter. The full record is just below.

How I teach

A lesson with me is not a coursebook turned page by page. We start from what you need to be able to do in Italian over the next few weeks, and choose what to study from there. Grammar arrives when it lets you say something, not before.

The first lesson is a free trial and it serves us both: you find out whether the approach suits you, I find out where you actually stand. I do not ask for a commitment before that.

What I have written

Three theses, and they are not a list of titles: they are where I tested the ideas I now use in lessons. If one of them interests you I am happy to send it, or to talk about it: write to me and say which.

L’importanza del dialogo interculturale in un corso LS blended attraverso la pubblicità e la musica

How to teach culture without reducing it to trivia. It lines up Bennett’s scale of intercultural sensitivity, Balboni’s model of communicative competence and the culture-shock curves of Oberg and Kim, and then puts them to work: a learning unit built on Italian songs and adverts in which Dutch learners compare stereotypes about Italy with their own.

ITALS Master, Ca’ Foscari University of Venice, 25th cycle, LS track, 2023/2024. Supervisor: Michele Blandino.

Considerazioni per una classe online di successo

What actually changes when a language lesson moves online. It starts from Krashen’s input hypothesis and the affective filter, that is from the fact that anxiety and embarrassment block learning before any grammatical difficulty does, and works out how to build a learning unit that holds up at a distance.

First-level master in teaching Italian as a second language, Università Giustino Fortunato, 2022/2023. Supervisor: Aurelio Liguori.

The Evolution of the Lingua Franca in Europe: From Latin to English

Why a language becomes everybody’s language, and why it later stops. A hundred and eighteen pages on Latin as the language of empire and knowledge, on the French of diplomacy, on Italian, which had cultural prestige without spread, and on today’s English. The chapter on Italian is why I teach with the history of the language next to the grammar.

Master’s degree thesis in Languages for International Communication, Università Mercatorum, 2024/2025. Supervisor: Emanuela Ammendola.
