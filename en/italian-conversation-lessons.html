Italian conversation lessons

For people who have the grammar on paper but seize up in a real conversation. This is the most requested track, and usually the most satisfying one.

Book a free intake

Why you freeze

Almost always for the same reason: you're translating in your head from English or Dutch, and that takes longer than a conversation allows. The fix isn't more grammar, it's more speaking time under mild pressure, with correction afterwards instead of mid-sentence.

How we work

Every lesson has a topic — current affairs, work, a film, an argument — and while you speak I keep a list of what went wrong. We go through it afterwards. That makes correction concrete without interrupting you mid-thought.

For advanced learners too

At B2 and C1 it's less about mistakes and more about register: the difference between correct Italian and Italian that sounds like an Italian. There we work on idiom, intonation, and the small words nobody teaches you.

Frequently asked questions

What level do I need?

From around A2. Below that there isn't enough material yet to build a conversation on, so we'd start with a mixed track.

Do you correct everything?

No, and that's deliberate. Constant interruption kills fluency. I take notes and we go through them afterwards.

Can I bring my own topics?

Please do. Lessons get better when they're about something you actually have an opinion on.

What do conversation lessons cost?

Same principle as the other tracks: set around your situation, discussed in the free intake.
