Online Italian lessons

Online I teach learners all over the world: from the Netherlands to Peru, Singapore and Canada. Same structure as in person, minus the travel.

Take the first step →︎

How it works technically

We work over video with a shared screen. I use interactive exercises, real texts, audio and images: not scanned textbook pages. Everything we cover gets sent to you after the lesson.

Time zones

I'm based in the Netherlands but also teach early mornings and evenings. In practice almost any time zone works; we'll find a slot in the intake that suits us both.

Let's talk →︎

Does online work as well?

For language, yes, and on one point it's actually better: I can pull up material, put a word on screen or play audio instantly without the lesson stalling. What you lose is the small talk on arrival, which is why every lesson deliberately opens with free conversation.

Frequently asked questions

What software do I need?

A browser and headphones. I send you a link; there's nothing to install.

What if I need to reschedule?

Let me know at least 24 hours ahead and we'll move it at no cost.

What does an online lesson cost?

Online is lower than in person, since there's no travel involved. The exact figure I set around your situation: we discuss it in the free intake.

About the lessons and prices

Lessons are taught by Alessio, a teacher of Italian as a foreign language with an ITALS master’s degree in teaching Italian abroad (Università Ca’ Foscari in Venice, LS track) and a master’s degree in teaching Italian as a second language (Università Giustino Fortunato in Benevento), teaching online and in person between Ede and Amsterdam since 2019. Prices are per person and include VAT where it applies; location, times and availability are agreed before we start. Thuis Italiaans is a sole proprietorship registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce. If I have no availability in a given period, I will put you in touch with another highly qualified teacher of Italian as a foreign language.
