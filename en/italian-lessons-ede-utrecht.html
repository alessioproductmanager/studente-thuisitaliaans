Italian lessons in Ede, Utrecht and Amersfoort

I live in Ede and teach in person across this region: Ede, Wageningen, Veenendaal, Amersfoort, Utrecht and everything in between.

Lessons close to home

On the Veluwe and around Utrecht the Italian offering is thin: usually just a weekly evening group course with a textbook that doesn't fit you. One-to-one at your home isn't only faster — it also saves you the trip into the city.

Families and children too

In this region I regularly teach families: a parent and child together, or two children at once. With children I work through games, images and movement — sitting and listening doesn't work.

Between lessons

You get access to over 700 free exercises on this site and to the ti app, so you keep practising on the days we don't meet.

Frequently asked questions

Do you come to Utrecht and Amersfoort?

Yes, both — and Wageningen, Veenendaal, Barneveld and Arnhem. For places further out we'll look together at whether online makes more sense.

Do you teach group lessons?

Small groups of two to four, if you already know each other and are at roughly the same level. I deliberately don't do large groups.

What if I've never studied Italian?

Then we start at A1 and build up calmly. Around half of my learners start completely from scratch.

What does a lesson in this region cost?

It depends on your situation: frequency, location, alone or as a pair. We'll talk it through in the free intake.
