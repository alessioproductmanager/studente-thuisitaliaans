Ciao, I'm Alessio

I teach Italian out of a deep passion for language, culture and history. After two specialised master's degrees in teaching Italian to speakers of other languages, I combine several methodologies into an approach built around the way you learn. My lessons are interactive and varied, with a real balance between theory and practice, online as well as in person.

Whether you travel to Italy regularly, live there, or simply dream of the language and the culture, you're welcome. My lessons leave room for real conversation: about yourself, travel, daily life, Italian culture, history or current affairs. That way you don't just learn the language — you learn to communicate with confidence.
