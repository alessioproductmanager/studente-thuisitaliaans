Italian for the permesso di soggiorno

The vocabulary that gets you through the residence permit paperwork without a translator.

The essential terms

  • Permesso di soggiorno — residence permit
  • Rinnovo — renewal / Prima emissione — first issue
  • Questura — police headquarters (where you go for immigration matters)
  • Prefettura — the office for citizenship files
  • Ufficio immigrazione — immigration office
  • Modulo — form / Kit postale — the pre-printed forms envelope from the post office
  • Marca da bollo — tax stamp (from tobacco shops — tabacchi)
  • Ricevuta — receipt (proof that you've applied — legally valid while you wait)
  • Appuntamento — appointment
  • Convocazione — official summons for your appointment
  • Fotosegnalamento — fingerprinting session at Questura

The phrases you'll say and hear

  • Ho un appuntamento alle 9 per il rinnovo. — I have a 9 o'clock appointment for the renewal.
  • Devo consegnare i documenti. — I need to submit the documents.
  • Ho fatto la domanda tramite kit postale. — I applied through the postal kit.
  • Ho ricevuto la convocazione. — I've received the summons.
  • Il permesso non è ancora pronto. — The permit isn't ready yet.
  • Ha portato la marca da bollo? — Did you bring the tax stamp?
  • Torni tra tre mesi. — Come back in three months.
