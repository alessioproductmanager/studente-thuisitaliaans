Italian body parts vocabulary

You'll need these at the pharmacy, the doctor, or when you're just describing a headache. Some plurals are weirder than they look.

Half of the A1 path is open, no sign-up

Start with A1 ↗︎

Head to toe

  • testa head / capelli hair / viso face
  • occhio eye (pl. occhi) / naso nose / bocca mouth / orecchio ear (pl. orecchie)
  • collo neck / spalla shoulder / schiena back / petto chest
  • braccio arm (pl. le braccia — feminine plural!) / mano hand (pl. le mani)
  • dito finger (pl. le dita — feminine plural!) / pancia belly
  • gamba leg / ginocchio knee (pl. le ginocchia) / piede foot (pl. i piedi)

Common phrases at the pharmacy

  • Mi fa male la testa. — I have a headache.
  • Ho mal di gola. — I have a sore throat.
  • Ho la febbre. — I have a fever.
  • Sono raffreddato/a. — I have a cold.
