Privacy & Legal Notes · Books

Last updated: July 2026

This page covers the book pages of the thuisitaliaans · Italian Graded Readers series on thuisitaliaans.com.

No data collection

The book pages contain no forms, require no registration and collect no personal data. We use no tracking or advertising cookies. For aggregate visitor statistics we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies, collects no personal data and does no fingerprinting.

Purchases on Amazon

The books are sold and distributed by Amazon. The buy buttons lead to Amazon’s website, where Amazon’s conditions of use and privacy notice apply. Payments, shipping, returns and invoicing are handled entirely by Amazon; thuisitaliaans receives and processes no data about your purchases.

Copyright

The adaptations, notes, learning materials and covers of the series are the work of Alessio Insegnante (thuisitaliaans) and are protected by copyright. The original texts being adapted are in the public domain.

For questions about this page, write to thuisitaliaans@gmail.com.
