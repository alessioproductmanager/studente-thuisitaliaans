Where to go next

Grammar sticks when you meet it again without looking for it: inside a story you want to finish, inside a sentence you actually need. That is why I rewrote around thirty Italian classics level by level, with notes alongside and no dictionary needed: at your level there is always one you can start tonight.

Every chapter in this section also has its own pathway inside the Ti app: the same topics, but inside the vocabulary of a world — the bar, transport, bureaucracy, football — with exercises that correct you instantly and explanations in your language too.

If you want someone to look at your mistakes, I teach lessons online and in person between Ede and Amsterdam. And if you don't know which level to start from, the level test tells you in ten minutes, free and without an account.

About the grammar section

The explanations and the exercises are free and need no account: your answers stay in your browser, we don’t see them and we don’t store them.
